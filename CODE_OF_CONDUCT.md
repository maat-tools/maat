# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

maat is a tool for understanding codebases. Contributions are evaluated on technical merit. We want the project to be a place where people can focus on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and specific. Vague praise and vague criticism are both unhelpful.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Technical disagreements are good; personal attacks are not.
- Keep discussions on-topic. Design debates belong in issues and ADRs, not in PR comment threads.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a private message to a maintainer via [github.com/maat-tools](https://github.com/maat-tools). Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or ban contributors at their discretion.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions) and when representing the project in public.
